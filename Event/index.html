Cromatech

Cromatech

Rules and Regulations:

  • Event conducted individually; no team participation allowed.
  • Theme strictly based on Technical Concepts & Mandala Art (Engineering,
    Technology, Innovation, Robotics, AI, Circuits, Machines, etc.).
  • Participants must bring their own drawing materials (sheet/chart,
    pencils, colors, brushes, shades, sketch pens, etc.).
  • Printed materials, tracing sheets, stencils, or electronic gadgets are
    prohibited.
  • Mobile phones and electronic devices are not allowed during the
    competition.
  • Time duration is 2 hours; no extra time provided.
  • Maintain discipline and silence throughout the competition.
  • Any malpractice, copying, or misconduct leads to immediate
    disqualification.
  • Judge's decision is final and binding.
